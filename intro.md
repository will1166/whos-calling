# Who's Calling

The house at the end of the street has been empty for months.

No lights. No movement. No reason for anyone to go inside.

You and your friends only came here because you thought the old stories were fake. Then the landline in the hallway starts ringing.

It rings once.

Then twice.

Then a voice says your name.

What do you do next?

You hang up the phone.
