# Follow the steps upstairs

You rush toward the stairs before anyone can stop you.

The steps creak beneath your feet as the sound of running suddenly stops.

Second floor.

Dead silence.

At the end of the hallway, a bedroom door slowly swings open on its own.

Inside, the room is covered in dust except for one thing:

A phone sitting on the bed.

Ringing.

One of your friends whispers, “Don’t answer it.”

But the moment the ringing stops, all your phones buzz at once.

Unknown Caller.

You answer before you can think.

Static crackles through the speaker.

Then your own voice whispers:

“You shouldn’t have come upstairs.”

The bedroom door slams shut.

And from the other side of it, something starts pounding to get out.
